## Changes and preservation

User requested larger iPad movement and JUMP buttons after a real-device screenshot.

Both stages now load one new, version-local stylesheet. On touch viewports at least 700px wide and 500px high, all four direction buttons are 88 x 88 CSS pixels and JUMP is 140 x 96. The direction grid itself is enlarged to matching 88px rows/columns with 10px gaps, rather than enlarging buttons inside the old 48px grid. The action-button container and landscape progress/message spacing are adjusted to avoid overlap. Tablet portrait and landscape are covered. Phone control dimensions remain unchanged.

Exactly four files were added under `review/v29-rc3/`. No existing file was modified or deleted. All 72 baseline files were checked byte-for-byte and preserved, including V28, RC2, the original homepage, shared CSS, engine and Pages workflow. All inline script contents in both copied stages are byte-identical to RC2. Gameplay, physics, levels, modes, records and the private V4.1 loader are unchanged. No private model or photograph was uploaded. Display labels identify RC3.

## Limits / acceptance still open

No physical iPad or phone was operated in this session. The private V4.1 asset was not available to these tests and was not loaded or published. No child enjoyment, long-session performance, full-route regression or WebKit multi-touch acceptance is claimed. This CSS-only release does not resolve or close unrelated RC2 issues, including the earlier unresolved multi-touch/tablet scenario; that scenario was not retested here.
