How to play

Hold the arrows to move. Tap JUMP to cross a gap and DASH for a short burst. Flags save your checkpoint; shields protect a life. Collect all five stars before entering the finish.

Practice lets you keep trying. Time Challenge records clean completed runs separately for each stage. Using Retry checkpoint turns that attempt into a practice finish; Restart begins a new timed attempt.
